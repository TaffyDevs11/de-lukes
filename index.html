Meet the artist

Luke Kamanga. Mbira forward.

De Lukes — born Luke Kamanga — is a Bulawayo singer, songwriter and mbira player whose music moves between RnB, Afrofusion, Soul and Gospel. His motto, Mbira Mberi ("mbira forward"), says it all: ancestral sound, carried boldly into the future.
